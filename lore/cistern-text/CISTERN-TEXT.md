# CISTERN-TEXT — plaques, gauges, notices, ledgers, graffiti, three vignettes

**Status: PROPOSAL.** Text only, original. Nothing here repeats a string from
`nullrun-text-20261101/INWORLD-TEXT.md`; the seven cistern plaques and three cistern
notices there can coexist with this set, one text per room. Read with `WAYFINDING.md`
(the signs that route) and `SOUND-CUES.md` (the pulls that lead).

**Placement rule.** One text per room. A plaque and a notice never share a wall.
Graffiti is never adjacent to the plaque that explains it. If a room has both a gauge
and a plaque, the gauge is the authored object and the plaque is the note beside it.

**The water, for consistency.** Works Zero is the sill of Sluice 1. High water is
`+2.40`, painted by hand and never repeated; the office's gauge still stands at that
mark. The water itself stands at `+1.95`. The growth scratched `+1.95` and `+1.60`, and
the office crossed the scratches out but did not paint over them. The cistern floor is
at about `+1.40`, so the line stands roughly 0.55 m deep in the gallery and deeper in
the sumps. Every tag in this file uses those marks.

## 3. Six graffiti and scratched tallies

Left by inhabitants who do not know they have stopped being people. None of them
recognises the change; each notices a detail — a cuff, a voice, a lap count — and
files it under routine. The generator must never let one of these become an
explanation.

## 4. Three vignette notes

Self-contained object scenes in the Unopened Tin / Private Mirror manner: one object,
one piece of writing, no explanation. The object is the room's only text; nothing else
in the room should be readable.

### THE UNOPENED TIN

**Where.** Store alcove, shelf above the line, between two empty brackets.
**Object.** Tall biscuit tin, 180 mm, solder-sealed, label gummed to the lid.
**Label.** `TIN — SEALED — 1 — CONTENTS NOT RECORDED — DO NOT ISSUE`
**Pencil on the lid.**
`Heavier than it was. Not opened. Kept closed for the assessor.`
**Staging.** The tin never opens. If it can be carried, it is the heaviest object of
its size in the level; do not explain the weight. The shelf label stays behind.

### THE PRIVATE MIRROR

**Where.** Pump chapel, bolted to a pipe at face height, above the line.
**Object.** Steel shaving mirror, 150 × 200 mm, one corner chipped. Dust above the
line; clean below it.
**Card tied to the bracket.** `PRIVATE — DO NOT CLEAN — DO NOT COVER`
**Pencil on the frame.**
`It keeps the room. It loses me. I have stopped asking it to.`
**Staging.** The mirror reflects the room, the water and the lamp. It never reflects
the player. No flicker, no sound, no jump. The note is the whole event.

### THE DRY COAT

**Where.** Gauge gallery, on a hook by the stair, above the line.
**Object.** Works coat, ochre, dry, size unmarked. Laundry mark in the collar:
`G1 — 3 — RD`.
**Note in the pocket, folded once.**
`COAT KEPT DRY FOR THE NEXT SHIFT. THE NEXT SHIFT IS LATE.`
**Staging.** It does not move when the player passes. The cistern's air moves
everything else in the room. Do not add a sound.

## 5. Use notes

- **R.D.** appears in the level log, the personnel transfer, the scratched roster and
  the coat. Four documents, one set of initials, never a name. Do not explain it in
  text; if the player builds a theory, the theory is theirs.
- **The gauge contradiction is the point.** The plaques say the water is stable; the
  scratches say it has been lower; the office has crossed out the scratches without
  painting over them. No document resolves which recorder is right. Do not add one.
- **Redactions** (`██`, `[ABRADED]`) read as paint, abrasion or a thumb, not as
  censorship.
- **Two jokes per room, maximum.** The dry ones are 19, 20, 21, 24 and 25. Keep them
  apart; none of them should share a wall with a body.
- **Depth tags** are local. `DEPTH 0.6 M` means at that door, at the current line; it is
  not a global sea level. The global marks are only the three on the staff gauge.
- **The tin, the mirror and the coat** are the only objects in this pack that imply a
  person. They never name one, and none of them is a body.
- **No text explains the flood.** Deliberate, accidental, or never a flood: the records
  disagree and the water does not take sides.
