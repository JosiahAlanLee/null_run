# RUN-STRUCTURE — a run is a case file

**Status: PROPOSAL.** Text only. Slay-the-Spire-legible: a run is short, seeded, and made of a few
meaningful choices; what persists between runs is knowledge, not power.

## 1. The frame: the descent is an intake case

You begin in **the waiting room** (stratum 0's receiving hall; the benches face the stairwell). The desk
issues your intake number — `NR-###-X` — and that number **is the run's seed**. You descend through the
five strata (transit → ossuary → scriptorium → cistern → root cathedral). The run ends at **the still
pool** (the bottom of stratum 4), and the ending is decided by your body (`BODY-BUDGET.md` §5) and
rendered at the desk as an intake form.

**Death** closes the case: `DECEASED.` You wake in the waiting room. The body is gone — every node,
material, mod and meter resets. The case is filed, and the file remembers.

## 2. Per stratum: six to nine rooms, four decisions

A stratum is a short descent with exactly four meaningful choices; everything else is rooms.

1. **The fork (route).** Two doors out of the landing. One route passes a **workbench** and its
   mechanical rooms (service corridors, works stores, pump rooms); the other passes a **nursery tray** and
   its organic rooms (the drying nursery, the basin edge, the grown bays). The fork decides which tree you
   can feed this stratum. Both stations exist in the descent; you can only reach one comfortably per
   stratum.
2. **The craft (one node).** Materials gathered in the stratum fund roughly **one node per stratum** — one
   bench install or one growth bedded. Materials are the pacing currency; the stations are the shops, and
   they sell different futures.
3. **The offering (price event, one per stratum).** Chosen suffering (PILLARS §4), always a fair price
   for a rare material, always skippable:
   - **S0 transit:** the photographer's drawer — take the photoform plate: **+2 drift**.
   - **S1 ossuary:** the shelf — give **10 max HP for the rest of the run** for a bronze cradle and a bone.
   - **S2 scriptorium:** the casting order — give up **one owned weapon** for 3 bronze plates.
   - **S3 cistern:** the gauge — **+2 pain** for the Spindle's large core (colony glass ×2).
   - **S4 root cathedral:** the still pool — give **15 max HP** for 2 colony glass.
4. **The gate.** At the bottom of each stratum: a seal room or set-piece (the Spindle's first infection
   is scripted here per ECOLOGY), or a quiet route — pay a price or make the passage unseen. The gate is
   where the stratum's discipline is tested.

**Landings** (rest stops between room chains): a bench, a stores locker, sometimes a station. The bench
seats you: health regenerates 1/s up to 50 (the Bier-Bench Plaque's rule, made a room feature). Sitting
is a physical act and the healing is slow (PILLARS §3).

## 3. What persists between runs: the FILE

The waiting-room desk holds your file. Persistence is knowledge, not power — the office keeps records,
not muscles.

1. **Node diagrams.** Every node you have ever built is drawn in the file's diagrams. On later runs its
   exact numbers are shown **before** you craft it. The first time you build a node you buy it blind; the
   file is how you learn.
2. **Filed endings.** Each ending reached once is filed and unlocks a start-of-run option at the desk:
   - after **any** filed ending: choose your starting weapon from those found in past runs;
   - after the **personnel** ending: choose your starting coat (Works Coat or Assessor's Coat);
   - after **three** filed endings: the office issues 2 common materials of your choice at the desk.
   That is all. No stats, no permanent nodes, no currency carry-over.
3. **The station ledger.** Stations you have used are marked on future maps **one room earlier** — a route
   hint, not a buff.

Everything else resets: the body is remade each run and re-paid for. The Purgatorio is not an
investment account.

## 4. Seeded strata

The seed (your case number) picks: which per-run history the strata tell (who sealed which door, whether
the cistern is full — tie to `GENERATORS.md`), which station sits on which fork, the offering contents,
the gate encounter, and the creature placement (spawn pairing rules always hold; the Hollow and the
ReDead always spawn together).

Same seed = the same descent (you may retry a case). New case = new descent. The departure board over the
desk lists the last three case numbers; the desk can reissue an old one. This keeps runs legible and
discussable: "case NR-311-R killed me in the cistern" is a sentence the game's fiction already wants.

## 5. The hub: the waiting room, and nothing else

The waiting room is the whole meta-game: the desk (file, unlocks, start kit), the departure board (seeds),
the stairwell mouth (begin the run), and the turnstile (the symbol the endings are judged against —
personnel pass it, the works body is listed on it, residents do not attempt it). Benches face the
stairwell. There is nothing to buy and nothing to grind; the hub is a desk and a decision, the way the
office would run it.

## 6. One run, at a glance

1. Waiting room: read the file, choose seed, choose starting kit (after unlocks). Descend.
2. Per stratum: fork → gather materials on the route → one craft at the station → the offering (maybe) →
   the gate. Landings between chains.
3. The still pool. The ending is decided by the body. The desk renders the intake form. The file updates.
4. Next case.
