## The set (a proposal about the set, not about the setting)

Two never-human organisms and one person, each a different medium of **continuity**: the Circuit is
continuity of connection (wire), the Underprint is continuity of touch (plaster and paint), and the Hem is
continuity of a person (wool and habit). Each remembers in its own material and none of them knows what it
is remembering. The sheets do not require that coherence; it is my proposal, offered because the three
sheets arrived as a batch and read as a family.
