# ITEMS — thirty proposed run upgrades

**Status: PROPOSAL for Josiah to accept or reject.** Relic-like modifiers for run-based progression (Slay-the-Spire-style build choices). Effects are written against the current game's systems — health, armour, ammunition, the 1–7 weapon slots, stagger windows, one life per run — but the game currently has no perk drafts, shops or unlocks; these are forward designs, not implemented content. Numbers are starting points for balance, not approved values.

Slot legend: **R** relic (passive, held for the run), **W** weapon charm (applies to the equipped weapon), **K** key item (also used in encounters). Drops are suggestions only.

---

### Stratum 0 — institutional transit

1. **Door 44 Schedule** — R, drops in transit service corridors.
   *Effect:* Doors that were locked at run start cost 0 keys to open; the run keeps a written list of every door you pass.
   *Lore:* Door 44 was installed and never ordered. The schedule is the only paper that knows which doors are real.

2. **Waiting-Room Slip** — R.
   *Effect:* Standing still for 3 seconds makes your next shot deal +50% damage (the office calls this "processing").
   *Lore:* The benches face the stairwell. The slip says your number will be called. It never is.

3. **Warning Tape, 40 Metres** — K.
   *Effect:* You may mark one door per floor; mimics and ambush closets behind it telegraph 1 extra second before acting.
   *Lore:* The tape is not for stopping it. The tape is for the next crew.

4. **Carbon Copy** — R.
   *Effect:* Every ammo pickup you take leaves a second copy for the next floor (carries once; 50% of the ammo, rounded down).
   *Lore:* Night shift pressed too hard, and the words went through. Everything here has a duplicate somewhere.

5. **The Desk That Faces the Stairs** — R.
   *Effect:* Enemies descending stairs toward you are visible through walls within 8 metres (desk-3 sightline).
   *Lore:* Personnel on desk 3 report that the stairs watch back. This is a lighting effect.

6. **Turnstile Token** — K, one use per floor.
   *Effect:* Resets one door or lift state in the current floor (seal, gate, vendor shutter) to its run-start condition.
   *Lore:* It turns once for each passenger and once for no one. The second turn is the gate remembering.

7. **Moth-Ridden Uniform** — R.
   *Effect:* Inhabitants that would attack on sight hesitate 1 second when you first enter their room, as if checking your name tag.
   *Lore:* You are wearing someone else's badge. It is still warm. Do not read the name aloud.

8. **The Hum, Two Keys** — W.
   *Effect:* Kills near light fittings restore 2 armour (the lower key hums; the upper key hums too).
   *Lore:* The upper key matches nothing on the maintenance bands. Tune nothing to it.

### Stratum 1 — limestone ossuary

9. **Kept-Dust** — K, consumable, 3 uses.
   *Effect:* Throw at a stone-faced enemy to blind it 3 seconds; masks, plaques and names within the cloud become legible on your map.
   *Lore:* Keep the names dusted. Do not read them aloud. Reading aloud is how they take the next one down.

10. **Cuff, Altered** — R.
    *Effect:* Wrist armour +1 per floor; at 4+ stacks you may re-roll the grip-escape check once per grab.
    *Lore:* The stitching improves each time. It fits nothing we have tried it on, and it is kept regardless.

11. **Shelf-Mark NR-077** — K.
    *Effect:* The first enemy that kills you in a run instead puts you at 1 health, shelves you in place, and walks away. (Once per run.)
    *Lore:* The name is still here. As long as it stays legible, the occupant knows which shelf is theirs.

12. **Bier-Bench Plaque** — R.
    *Effect:* While seated (any usable chair), health regenerates 1/sec up to 50.
    *Lore:* The benches face the wrong way to watch. They face the way you watch when you are waiting to be collected.

13. **Two-Hand Dusting Rota** — R.
    *Effect:* +15% damage with the first weapon slot after you have fired every other owned weapon once this floor.
    *Lore:* Rota to be initialled by both hands on the ladder. Both hands must agree.

14. **Votive Receipt** — R.
    *Effect:* Vendors give one free basic purchase per floor (the coins are already spent).
    *Lore:* The lamp has been burning for longer than the office has kept receipts. Do not ask for the coins back.

### Stratum 2 — bronze scriptorium

15. **Sleeve 6** — R.
    *Effect:* Weapon switch speed +30%; switching weapons mid-stagger keeps your stagger bonus one extra hit.
    *Lore:* Return sleeve 6 to Room 6. Room 6 has been re-numbered Room 6.

16. **Overstruck Correction** — W.
    *Effect:* Your first miss each combat does not consume ammunition, and re-fires instantly at -20% damage ("the correction is cast deeper than the error").
    *Lore:* The error is part of the correction. Both readings are precedents.

17. **Casting Order** — K.
    *Effect:* Pay 50% of current ammo to cast a bronze copy of one owned weapon's next shot: it repeats after 4 seconds, once, wherever you stand then.
    *Lore:* The scrap remembers the first reading. That is why we do not melt the scrap.

18. **The Empty Helmet** — R.
    *Effect:* Your first shot after entering a room announces itself: enemies in the room turn toward the sound instead of you for 2 seconds.
    *Lore:* An answer is treated as an audience. The helmet is used to being the one that speaks.

19. **Margin Colophon** — R.
    *Effect:* Map edges are annotated one room beyond the explored area (doors, stairs, water, seals only).
    *Lore:* This wall continues on the next wall. The margin is full.

20. **Lamp Label** — K, reusable.
    *Effect:* Reading a plaque or terminal within 5 seconds of a kill restores 5 health and makes the next floor's maps start 10% explored.
    *Lore:* After the third hour, the names read themselves. They do not need the light for it.

### Stratum 3 — flooded cistern

21. **The Level Line** — R.
    *Effect:* You take no damage from standing water; walking along the waterline (the clean ring) at a walk speed gives +20% move speed.
    *Lore:* Above this line: dry. Below this line: not dry. If the line moves, you are not where you were.

22. **Gauge, Highest Mark** — R.
    *Effect:* Enemy health bars show as a filled gauge (qualitative: full/half/low) for one enemy type per floor, chosen by the gauge.
    *Lore:* Gauges are read, not believed. The gauge has been at the highest mark for the whole period the logs cover.

23. **Dedication Plate** — K.
    *Effect:* Place it on a wall to flood one adjacent room 20 seconds later, lowering enemy sightlines and your own fire height. (One use per floor.)
    *Lore:* Only one plate is above the water. The other one thanks the pipework.

24. **Pest Ledger** — R.
    *Effect:* Kills of small organisms (spore, mite, growth) add +1% damage for the rest of the run, to a cap of +20%.
    *Lore:* The count never comes out the same. Something puts them back.

25. **Boat Chit** — K, one use per floor.
    *Effect:* Summon the boat at any causeway edge; it carries you across one body of water in 4 seconds, exposed but quiet.
    *Lore:* The oars were borrowed by the stairs. The boat makes a sound the water repeats, twice, softer.

26. **Grate Punctuation** — R.
    *Effect:* Passing through a grate or duct costs no health and makes no noise for 3 seconds after.
    *Lore:* The growth passes through them politely, the way a reader turns a page.

### Stratum 4 — root cathedral

27. **The Correct Reply** — K.
    *Effect:* In any scripted exchange (plates, bells, heralds), one wrong input per exchange is forgiven and re-demonstrated. (Does not reveal answers.)
    *Lore:* The sequence was not written down. The plate shows it anyway, worn by repetition.

28. **Chair-Leg Note** — R.
    *Effect:* Sitting in any chair for 6 seconds tells you the room's ambient danger (none/hidden/armed) through the creak, once per room.
    *Lore:* The creak is different every time, which means it is a reply, which means someone is home.

29. **Grown Ladder** — K, reusable.
    *Effect:* Plant it at any vertical drop; a cable-and-rebar ladder grows in 3 seconds, usable once, then becomes scenery.
    *Lore:* The rungs are spaced for someone still learning what hands are for. Do not thank the ladder.

30. **The Descent Remembering Itself** — R, one of three per run.
    *Effect:* Choose a stratum at pickup: for the rest of the run, you deal +12% damage there and enemies from that stratum deal -12% to you. The cathedral remembers your choice in its ribs.
    *Lore:* The inscription is still legible if you read it the way the ribs grow, which is the way water remembers a level.

---

**Balance notes (proposal):** relics stack; K items are floor-scoped consumables unless noted; all numbers are starting values for Josiah's tuning. The last item is the only one that references the run's structure directly, and it should be tested last.
