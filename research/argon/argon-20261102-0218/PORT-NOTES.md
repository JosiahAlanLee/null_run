- #17 +SETTLEDEBRIS thinker: once sleep_tics >= 6, unlink the debris actor from ThinkerList[STAT_ACTORMOVER], waking it from P_CheckPosition when a bounding box overlaps its cell.
- #19 sector_t::GravityDir for E10: gravityx/y/z UDMF sector properties (default (0,0,-1)); multiply mo->Vel by R_grav^T at the start of P_XYMovement / P_ZMovement, and pre-multiply the GL view matrix by R_grav^T.
