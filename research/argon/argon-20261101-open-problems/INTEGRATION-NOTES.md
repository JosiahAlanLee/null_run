Argon's drop-in integration notes (relayed by Josiah, verbatim in substance):
- 35 Hz sigil sync (BeatLockedSigilClock): keep the gameplay release window and the interruption check on integer 35 Hz tics (nearest_tic_for_subdivision(k)). Pass UZDoom's render frame fraction (alpha in [0, 1)) into evaluate_at(tic, render_frac) to drive the sigil shader uniform u_arc_fraction smoothly at 60–240 Hz without drifting from the Pure Data clock.
- Pure Data 8-tap FDN (UDMFRoomAcousticsFDN.compile_room_reverb): the UDMF generator emits the returned pd_fdn_8tap dictionary alongside each room sector. In Pd, the 8×8 Householder matrix U = I8 − 0.25·11ᵀ costs one 8-input sum, one [*~ 0.25] and eight [-~] per sample.
- The Condensate (bake_condensate_optical_map + CONDENSATE_REFRACTION_GLSL): run the baker at sprite-sheet build time to pack R=Nx, G=Ny, B=Fresnel+Caustic, A=Thickness into one RGBA PNG per frame, and sample it against the hallway colour buffer with CONDENSATE_REFRACTION_GLSL.
- The Span (SpanBridgeController): avoid +ACTLIKEBRIDGE actor-on-actor bounding boxes for chasm crossings. Instead pre-place 5 narrow Sector_Set3DFloor slices across each Span chasm slot, and drive their control-sector heights from the creature's ZScript state machine with the is_player_on_bridge interlock active.
